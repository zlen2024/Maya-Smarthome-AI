One home, every room

Your house, brought to life.

Group your smart sockets by room and see the whole home at a glance. Flip a single channel, or hit “All off” on your way out the door.

  • 🏠
    Multi-house. Switch between homes — apartment, parents’ place, the office — in a tap.
  • ⚡
    Instant. Every toggle broadcasts over WebSockets, so every phone stays in sync.
See it in action

Control that feels instant.

Sign in, tap a channel, watch it flip — the socket responds and every other device updates live. No refresh, no lag.

  • 🔌
    3-channel sockets. Name each outlet — “PS5”, “TV”, “Lamp” — and control them one by one.
  • 🌙
    All off. One button cuts every active channel across the house.
  • 📡
    Live status. Online / offline and channel state, always current.
Meet Maya

Just ask.

Maya is your household’s AI assistant, right inside the chat. Ask in plain language — English or Malay — and she runs it. The whole family shares one house conversation.

  • 💬
    Natural language. “bukak tv”, “turn everything off” — Maya understands.
  • @
    Mentions. Tag @Maya or a family member in the shared house chat.
@Maya bukak tv 13:31
✦ Maya

Real hardware

Powered by ESP32.

Every Maya socket runs on an ESP32 — provisioned over Bluetooth in seconds, then online over Wi-Fi. Open firmware, open API, yours to build on.

  • 📡
    BLE provisioning. Scan, set Wi-Fi, done — no cloud-account juggling.
  • 🔓
    Open API. Wire Maya into your own automations.
